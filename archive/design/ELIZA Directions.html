Two directions to compare

A fresh brand and visual system for ELIZA — Elan Expo's internal operating platform. Both directions are professional, calm, international, and engineered for dense daily work; they diverge on temperature and personality. Each is applied to the two anchor screens you asked for.

How to read this

  • Direction A — Signal: cool, precise, "control-room." Slate + ink-blue with a live cyan accent; geometric type. Reads as a serious operations console.
  • Direction B — Atlas: warm, editorial, premium. Ivory paper + forest green with an amber data accent; serif headlines. Reads as a trustworthy system of record.
  • Both carry the ELIZA wordmark + logomark, the role-gated Sales / Operations / Finance top nav (with the small liffy / leena sub-labels), and one shared component set.
